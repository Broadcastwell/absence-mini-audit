      Questions about the Free 10-question check

      What does the Free 10-question check (one engine) measure?

      Whether Perplexity names your company when a buyer asks ten shortlist questions in your category: three best of, three alternatives, two comparison, one evaluation and one use case. Each answer is marked named or not named, and the count places you on the published absence ladder.

      Is it free, and do I need an email?

      It is free. No email is needed; runs are limited per network and per day, and by the site's daily allowance.

      Why do you read my website?

      To propose your company name and your category in a buyer's words, so the only thing you type is your address. We read your homepage and at most two more public pages, with a user agent that names Broadcastwell, and keep nothing we read. Nothing runs until you confirm.

      How is it different from the $490 Category Audit?

      The Category Audit is ten questions, five engines, three runs: 150 scheduled observed answers and three prioritised fixes. Findings within 48 hours of the category being confirmed. It also shows who is named instead of you and the sources visible in those answers.

      Does one run tell me where I stand?

      It is one run on one engine, and answers vary between runs. That is why the paid measurements ask every question three times on every engine and report the disagreement.

      What does a result link keep, and for how long?

      Only what the wheel shows: your company name, website, category, the ten questions and their marks. No email and nothing about you. It is kept for 30 days and then deleted.
